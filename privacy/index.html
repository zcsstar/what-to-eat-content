隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日

「吃啥」（英文名 What to Eat?）是个人开发者 Chi Zhang 开发的家常菜推荐 App。本页说明这个 App 会处理哪些数据。

简要说明

  • 不需要注册账号。开发者没有自己的服务器，不收集你的个人信息，App 里也没有统计分析工具。
  • 你在 App 里填的内容只保存在这台设备上，不会上传：家里有的食材、过敏原和忌口、不喜欢的菜、收藏、评分、做过的菜、保存的套餐和各项设置。
  • App 通过 Google AdMob 展示广告来保持免费，AdMob 会按下面所说收集一些设备信息。
  • 菜谱更新从 GitHub Pages 下载，App 更新通过 Expo 的更新服务下载；这些请求不带你的个人信息。

保存在你设备上的数据

上面这些内容存在 App 自己的存储空间里，下载的菜谱和照片也缓存在设备上。开发者看不到这些数据，也不会把它们用于广告。删除 App 就会全部清除。

广告（Google AdMob）

按照 Google 的说明，AdMob 的软件包会自动收集：

  • IP 地址，可能用来估计设备的大致位置；
  • 设备标识符：Android 上是广告 ID 和 app set ID；iOS 上 App 不请求跟踪权限，所以 AdMob 拿不到广告标识符（IDFA），但可能使用只在本 App 或本开发者范围内有效的设备标识符；
  • 广告和使用信息，比如看过的广告、点击、视频观看和 App 启动；
  • 诊断信息，比如 App 的启动时间、卡顿和耗电情况，以及崩溃日志。

这些数据用于投放和统计广告、控制同一广告出现的次数以及防止作弊，由 Google 处理，适用 Google 隐私权政策和Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。开发者拿不到这些数据。你在 App 里填的过敏原、忌口等信息不会提供给 AdMob，也不会用于投放广告。

你的选择

  • 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士：App 会显示 Google 的同意表单，你可以同意、拒绝或逐项选择。之后可以在 App 的「设置 › 隐私选项」里随时更改。
  • 美国设有相关隐私法的州：可以在「设置 › 隐私选项」里选择不出售或分享你的个人信息。
  • Android：可以在系统设置的「广告」选项里删除或重置广告 ID。
  • iOS：App 不请求跟踪权限，不会进行跨 App 跟踪。

菜谱更新和 App 更新

  • 菜谱和照片的更新从 zcsstar.github.io（GitHub Pages）下载。和访问任何网站一样，GitHub 会收到你的 IP 地址和设备的基本信息，适用 GitHub 隐私声明。App 不会发送任何标识符或个人信息。
  • App 的程序更新通过 Expo 的更新服务（EAS Update）下载。请求里有 App 版本、系统平台，以及更新组件在设备上生成的随机安装 ID，用于发放和统计更新，适用 Expo 隐私政策。

儿童

App 适合所有年龄使用，但不是专门面向儿童的服务，开发者不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

权限

App 只用网络访问，用来下载菜谱更新、App 更新和广告。不用相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录或照片。分步模式会让屏幕保持常亮，这不需要额外权限。

数据保留与删除

开发者不保存你的任何数据。设备上的数据在删除 App 时一并删除。Google、GitHub 和 Expo 的数据保留按它们各自的政策执行。

政策变更

如果 App 增加了会改变数据处理方式的功能（比如统计分析或账号），本页会更新，并修改上面的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

吃啥 (What to Eat?) is a home-cooking app by the individual developer Chi Zhang. This page explains what data the app handles.

Summary

  • No account. The developer runs no servers of their own and collects no personal information, and the app has no analytics.
  • What you enter stays on this device and is never uploaded: the ingredients you have, your allergens and foods you avoid, dishes you dislike, favourites, ratings, the dishes you have cooked, saved meals and your settings.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob to stay free; AdMob collects some device data, as described below.
  • Recipe updates come from GitHub Pages and app updates from Expo's update service; these requests carry no personal information.

Data stored on your device

The items above live in the app's own storage, along with the recipes and photos it downloads. The developer can't see this data and never uses it for advertising. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

According to Google, the AdMob SDK automatically collects:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location;
  • device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iOS the app doesn't ask for tracking permission, so AdMob gets no advertising identifier (IDFA), but it may use identifiers limited to this app or developer;
  • ad and usage information, such as the ads you see, taps, video views and app launches;
  • diagnostics, such as launch time, hangs and energy use, and crash logs.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often the same ad appears and prevent fraud, under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The developer has no access to it. Your allergens, foods you avoid and other entries are never passed to AdMob or used to target ads.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent form, where you can consent, refuse or choose per purpose, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information in Settings › Privacy options.
  • Android: you can delete or reset the advertising ID in the system's Ads settings.
  • iOS: the app doesn't ask for tracking permission and doesn't track you across apps.

Recipe and app updates

  • Recipe and photo updates are downloaded from zcsstar.github.io (GitHub Pages). As with any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The app sends no identifiers or personal information.
  • App updates are downloaded from Expo's update service (EAS Update). The request carries the app version, the platform and a random installation ID that the update component creates on the device, used to deliver and count updates, under the Expo Privacy Policy.

Children

The app suits all ages but isn't directed at children, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The app uses only network access, to download recipe updates, app updates and ads. It doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Step-by-step mode keeps the screen on, which needs no permission.

Retention and deletion

The developer keeps none of your data. The data on your device is deleted with the app. Google, GitHub and Expo keep data under their own policies.

Changes

If the app gains a feature that changes how data is handled, such as analytics or accounts, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
